NAMING RULE
One grammar for every file in this archive. Applied 2026-09-07 to all 96
media files. Anything added later follows the same rule.

THE PATTERN
  <index>_<subject>[_<qualifier>][_<token>].<extension>

  index       Two digits, zero padded, sequential within its own folder,
              starting at 01. Assigned once and never changed. New files
              take the next free number rather than being slotted in.

  subject     What the file shows. Lower case, words joined by hyphens.
                filter-plate-kripsol, manifold-four-drops, level-column

  qualifier   Optional, and only when a file needs distinguishing from the
              one beside it. Says what makes it different, not what it is.
                annotated, angled, on-vessel, stitched, single-frame

  token       Optional, always last, and always shaped so it cannot be read
              as an ordinary word:
                tHHMM      capture time on a 24 hour clock  (photos, video)
                WIDTHxHEIGHT   pixel size                   (panoramas)
                en es fr   language of the document         (manuals)
                           es-en means the document carries both

  extension   Lower case.

SEPARATORS
  Underscore between fields. Hyphen between words inside a field.
  Never a space. Never a capital letter. Never a full stop except before
  the extension.

WHY IT LOOKS LIKE THIS
  The index makes the sort order match the order things were shot or the
  order water flows through them, so a directory listing reads as a
  sequence rather than an alphabet. The token is last and always marked, so
  a four digit number can only ever be a clock time and never part of a
  name. Two photographs of the same thing carry the same subject and are
  separated by their index, not by a letter glued onto the end.

THE FOLDERS
  00_inbox          New material, unsorted, named however it arrived.
  01_survey         The deliverable. Exempt from the index rule: these three
                    files keep stable descriptive names because the README
                    and anyone you send this to point straight at them.
  02_printed-sheets The four printed pages from the room, photographed.
  03_nameplates     Plates and labels. Ordered along the water path:
                    filter, valve, pump, then the chemical.
  04_plant-room     Photographs of the plant room, in the order taken.
  05_balance-room   Photographs of the balance tank and the hatch that
                    reaches it, plus ANNOTATION-KEY.txt recording what the
                    coloured marks on photograph 04 mean.
  06_drawings       The owner's own drawings: the dimensioned sketch of the
                    plant room, and the scaled plan of both rooms together.
  07_video          Phone pans.
  08_panoramas      Stitched and wide images.
  09_manuals        The makers' own documents, plus SOURCES.txt giving the
                    official link for each. Ordered along the water path.
  11_scan           A 3D scan of the plant room, plus the plan derived from
                    it. READ-ME.txt records what its geometry confirms and
                    what it must not be used for.
  10_products       Every chemical on the shelf, photographed with its label,
                    grouped by function rather than by the order shot, plus
                    CATALOGUE.txt: what each one is, its active ingredient,
                    its dose worked out for this pool, its hazards, and a
                    symptom-to-action guide.

  Folder numbers are a reading order. File indices are permanent and
  append-only, because things get cited by them ("photograph 34"); folder
  numbers are not cited that way, so a new category may be inserted in the
  middle where it belongs, and the folders after it renumbered.

DELETED INDICES
  An index is never reused, so a gap in a folder means something was removed
  rather than that something is missing. This is the register of those gaps.
  It is deliberately terse: where a deletion carries a lesson, the lesson
  lives in that folder's own READ-ME and this list only points at it.

    04_plant-room/28   2026-09-08  A second frame of the pump strainer lid,
                       taken in the same second as 27 and identical but for

A NOTE ON TIME TOKENS
  For photographs the tHHMM token comes from EXIF capture time. PNG files
  carry no EXIF, so for those it comes from the file's modification time,
  which for a drawing exported straight from the phone is the same thing to
  within a minute.

SECOND PASS, 2026-09-07 EVENING
  Folder numbers were single digits, while file indices were two. That breaks
  the moment there are more than ten folders, because "10_" sorts before
  "9_". Padded every folder to two digits, so Products can arrive as
  10_products with nothing to renumber.
  A 06_drawings folder was added between the two rooms and the media that
  follows, and the plant room's dimensioned sketch moved into it: it had been
  filed as 04_plant-room/35, which made a folder of photographs contain
  something that is not one. 04_plant-room is 34 photographs again.

THIRD PASS, 2026-09-07 EVENING

  the next free folder number exactly as the padding was meant to allow.
