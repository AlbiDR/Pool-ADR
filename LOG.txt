ACTIVITY LOG

What was actually done, and when. The survey describes the installation; this
records its operation. They answer different questions, and the survey kept
sending you to a place that did not exist: "write down what you added", "write
down the gauge reading after that last backwash". This is that place.

Why it exists, concretely. On 2026-09-08 the question "how long has the
reintegro actually run?" could not be answered. Not because nobody knew, but
because the only record was a conversation, and a conversation records when
things were SAID, not when they were DONE. Two wrong estimates were built on
that gap before anyone noticed. A fill that has been paused twice looks
identical, in hindsight, to one that ran straight through.


FORMAT
  One line per event. Newest at the TOP, so appending is a single line and the
  recent state is the first thing you see.

      YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM  CATEGORY   what happened

  Times are local wall clock, CEST in summer and CET in winter. Do not convert
  anything; write the time on the clock in front of you.

  CATEGORY is one of:

      LEVEL       pool level, or a reading off the clear column
      REINTEGRO   top-up switched, and to which position (0, A, M)
      PUMP        pump switched, and to which position
      KRIPSOL     six-way lever moved, and to which number
      VALVE       any valve opened or shut, by its letter
      GAUGE       filter pressure reading, in bar
      BACKWASH    backwash and rinse performed
      DOSE        a chemical added, with the amount
      SALT        salt added or bought, with the amount
      TEST        a water test result
      SERVICE     maintenance done: strainer, O-ring, probe calibration
      NOTE        anything else worth knowing later

  Write the number, always. "topped up the chlorine" is worth much less in six
  months than "160 g granular". An entry with no figure in it is usually a NOTE.


HOW MUCH TO WRITE
  Every switch of the reintegro or the pump. Every dose. Every backwash and the
  gauge reading after it. Every level measurement taken with a tape rather than
  an eye. That is enough to reconstruct anything later, and little enough that
  it takes ten seconds.

  If you did something and did not log it, log it late rather than not at all,
  and mark the time as approximate with a "~".
