THE FOUR PRINTED SHEETS FROM THE ROOM

These are the household reference: four printed A4 pages kept in the plant room
and used to run this pool for years. The whole procedures section of the survey
is transcribed and translated from them, keeping their own numbering.

  01, 02   Sheet 1   PRINCIPIO DI FUNZIONAMENTO IN ESERCIZIO
  03       Sheet 2   TRATTAMENTO ELETTROLISI AL SALE
  04       Sheet 3   PULIZIA
  05       Sheet 4   CONTROLLO ACQUA

  Five files for four sheets: 01 and 02 are the same page, square on and at an
  angle. Sheet 1 is the one everything else refers back to, so it is worth
  having twice.

WHY THESE MATTER MORE THAN THEIR STATUS SUGGESTS
  Sheet 1 is where the valve letters come from. A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, L, M
  and the sump N are not tags invented for the survey; they are the vocabulary
  already written on this page and already spoken in the room. The survey uses
  them for exactly that reason, so the drawing, the sheets and anything said
  out loud stay in one language.

WHERE THEY ARE WRONG, AND WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT
  They are a household reference, not a specification, and two things on them
  do not survive contact with the makers' own manuals:

  THE GAUGE.  The sheet says "about 1 bar", and MEASUREMENT ON 2026-09-09 PROVED
              THE SHEET RIGHT: 1.18 bar on freshly backwashed clean sand.
              Kripsol's manual gives 0.5 to 0.7
  bar as normal running and 1.0 as the point at which you stop and backwash.
  The survey follows the manual and says so where it matters.

  THE DRUM.  The fifth note on sheet 1 calls the container "il bidone del
  cloro", the chlorine drum. It is not chlorine. The label reads ACIDO
  SOLFORICO 36% and is photographed in 03_nameplates/05. On a salt pool the
  chlorine is made by the cell; the drum is the pH minus the TRi PRO doses.
  That wording is the kind of thing that gets a chlorine product stood next to
  an acid drum, which is the one combination in this building that produces a
  toxic gas. It is worth correcting the print-out by hand.

  Nothing on the sheets has been edited here. The photographs are as found, and
  the corrections live in the survey where they can be argued with.

A NOTE ON 05
  Sheet 4 came off the phone tagged "Rotate 270 CW". That tag is correct and
  the pixels are the original bytes off the camera, so it renders upright in
  anything that reads EXIF: Preview, Finder, every browser, and the contact
  sheet in this folder. It appears on its side only in software that ignores
  the tag. It has deliberately not been re-encoded to straighten it, because
  that would trade real provenance for the convenience of tools that are the
  ones in the wrong. MEDIA.tsv records the tag so nobody is surprised by it.
