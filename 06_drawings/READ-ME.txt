WHAT IS TO SCALE AND WHAT IS NOT

  The two ROOMS are to scale, and it has been checked rather than assumed.
  Measured off the drawings at one consistent scale:

      technical room width     2.55 m       drawn 2.552 m
      technical room depth     1.98 m             1.979 m
      notch width              1.60 m             1.600 m
      notch depth              0.30 m             0.304 m
      right-hand projection    0.96 m             0.952 m
      balance room width       0.91 m             0.911 m
      balance room length      3.00 m             2.996 m

  The SERVICE CORRIDOR is NOT measured. The owner said so, and the drawing
  agrees: its horizontal leg scales to about 0.47 m deep, which is not a
  width anyone walks down. Take the corridor as topology only, meaning which
  way it runs and where its openings are, never as a dimension.

THE LABEL THAT READS WRONG

  "HATCH TO TECHNICAL ROOM" appears twice in 04, once on the balance room's
  far end and once on the top of the corridor's vertical leg. Those are the
  two sides of one wall, and one hatch through it. Neither side is the
  technical room: the hatch joins the SERVICE CORRIDOR to the BALANCE ROOM.
  That matches the entrance photograph, 05_balance-room/01, which shows the
  hatch high in the end wall of a narrow corridor. Read the label as marking
  where the hatch is, not where it goes.

A NOTE ON PROVENANCE
