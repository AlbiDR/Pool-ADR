HOW THIS ARCHIVE GOT ITS SHAPE

The rule itself is in NAMING.txt. This is the record of the restructurings that
produced it, kept because each one was a decision with a reason, and a reason
that is not written down gets undone by the next person who cannot see it.

Newest first, like LOG.txt. LOG.txt records what was done to the POOL; this
records what was done to the ARCHIVE.

2026-09-10  THE POOL GOT A FOLDER, AND SIX FOLDERS MOVED UP ONE
===============================================================================
  The archive documented the plant room, the balance room, the drawings, the
  manuals, the chemicals and a 3D scan, and held not one photograph of the
  pool. Two arrived, taken 52 seconds apart from opposite ends on 2026-09-09,
  and there was nowhere to put them. 04_plant-room would have been wrong and
  05_balance-room worse.

  SO THE POOL GOT A FOLDER, AND IT WENT IN THE MIDDLE. NAMING.txt already
  allowed for this: file indices are permanent and append-only because the
  survey cites them by number, but folder numbers are only a reading order,
  so a new category may be inserted where it belongs and the folders after it
  renumbered. The pool belongs with the two rooms, after them, so it is
  06_pool and 06_drawings through 11_scan became 07 through 12. Six git mv
  operations and 171 path references rewritten across fourteen files.

  Earlier CHANGELOG entries were rewritten too, so their paths still resolve.
  That trades a little historical literalness for links that work, which is
  the right way round here: the folders are the same folders, and an entry
  pointing at 08_panoramas when no such directory exists helps nobody. Where
  a number is quoted below in an older entry, it is the number that folder
  carries now.

  WHAT THE POOL PHOTOGRAPHS ARE FOR, AND WHAT THEY ARE NOT. They show the
  shape, the layout, and the overflow edge running under the deck on the
  hedge side, which is the whole reason there is a balance tank next door.
  They also show why this water cannot be judged by looking at it: both
  frames read as a black mirror, with the clouds sharper on the surface than
  anything beneath it.

  They must never be measured. Both were shot on the phone's ultra-wide, and
  that is now established rather than reported: the EXIF says 2.15 mm at
  f/2.2, an 18 mm equivalent, which is the 0.6x lens. At that focal length
  the near deck stretches, the far end compresses and parallels converge
  hard. This is not a theoretical caution. A pool volume was once read off
  these very frames, came out roughly a third low, and was withdrawn. The
  folder's READ-ME, its caption, the folder table and the photo index all say
  so.

  One file was deliberately not kept. A clarity frame from the same
  afternoon, looking straight down at the white floor grate, was the visual
  record behind the 2026-09-09 12:30 TEST entry. It was deleted at the
  owner's request. The cost is recorded in 06_pool/READ-ME.txt: the log's
  first clarity reading now has no image behind it, and the baseline every
  later reading would have been compared against has to be reshot.

  Also swept up: 04_plant-room/READ-ME.txt still said thirty-three
  photographs and the survey's folder table said thirty-four. There are
  thirty-six, and the session list now names the night pass, 35 to 37, that
  produced them.

2026-09-10  A SHARPER PANEL PHOTOGRAPH, AND WHAT IT SETTLED
===============================================================================
  Two photographs of the electrical panel arrived, shot at 01:47 and 01:48,
  and they are the reason this entry exists. They came in as 35 and 36, and
  35 is sharper than 20 in the way that matters: every legend strip under
  both rows is legible, and the component labels can be read rather than
  guessed at.

  SO IT REPLACED 20 WHERE 20 WAS DOING WORK. The inline figure in the survey,
  the "the ones everything else rests on" list in 04_plant-room/READ-ME.txt,
  and the equipment table now all point at 35. 20 stays on disk and keeps its
  index: it is dated 2026-09-06 and records where every switch stood before
  the system was drained, which 35 cannot show. The rule that produced this,
  and the order to do it in, is now written down in MAINTENANCE.md under
  "When a better photograph arrives", so it does not have to be rediscovered.

  WHAT 35 SETTLED. The panel carries two Lovato LVM 20 level relays for
  conductive liquids, and the legend under the second one reads CONTROLLO
  LIVELLO REINTEGRO. The top-up's automatic control is therefore a level
  relay and not a timer. That question had been open since 2026-09-06, and it
  was open in a specific and awkward way: the phrases "stops automatically"
  and "self-terminates" were both on the NEVER list precisely because nobody
  had verified them.

  Retiring a ban needed somewhere to put it. Deleting the two lines would
  have lost the reason they were ever in doubt, which is the one thing this
  archive tries hardest not to do. FACTS.txt now has a SETTLED block above
  NEVER, carrying both retired lines, the evidence that retired them, and the
  uncertainty that survives: no wiring diagram has been seen, and the probe
  positions and set levels are still unknown. It sits above the NEVER header
  rather than below it because C11 reads everything after that header as a
  ban, and a retired entry filed there would be enforced as one.

  It also corrected a component that is not in this panel. The equipment
  table said "Two Lovato LVM 20 and LVM 25 level relays". There is no LVM 25.
  Both are LVM 20, and the survey now names the rest of the gear from the
  same frame: MN513N motor protector, ESC 225 contactor, SVN 126 indicator,
  EH011 time switch, ST314 transformer, LS501 fuse holders, CDC 724H RCD,
  MJN516A MCB.

  AND IT CAUGHT SOMETHING NOBODY WAS LOOKING FOR. The pump clock is the EH011
  in the upper row, under PROG. POMPA. Its index sat near 22:00 in a frame
  whose EXIF says 01:47: about four hours slow. These are synchronous motor
  clocks, they stop whenever the panel is switched off, they never catch up,
  and this panel is switched off for every Kripsol lever move. So the A
  position has been running the pump at hours nobody chose, and it will drift
  again after the next backwash. That is now an open item, and the survey
  says to read the clock before trusting A.

  36 opened a question rather than closing one. It is the round 24 h rider
  dial in the LOWER row, riders pushed down from 2 to 21, and no legend strip
  claims it. It is not the filtration pump, because that is the EH011. What
  it switches is recorded as unknown, with the test for finding out, and the
  caption on 22 was corrected: it had asserted this dial ran the pump.

  One thing was deliberately not done. Both files were copied in byte for
  byte and the hashes checked against the originals, because this library was
  "optimised" once before and most of it came back unusable. That is now rule
  zero of the same MAINTENANCE.md section: in an archive the pixels are the
  evidence, a smaller file is not a better one, and the only re-encoded
  copies that belong anywhere are the ones the survey generates for itself.

2026-09-09  THE MEDIA LIBRARY GOT AN INDEX
===============================================================================
  Ninety media files, cited by number all through the survey, and no way to
  answer "what is photograph 17?" except by opening a 3 MB file and looking.
  04_plant-room, the folder nearly every factual claim rests on, had 33
  photographs and no index of any kind.

  MEDIA.tsv is now the one home for what each file is: one row per file, with
  its size, pixel dimensions, capture time and sha256 computed from the file
  itself, and a one-line caption written by hand. tools/media.py maintains it
  and C21 fails when it drifts, so a file cannot arrive without a caption and
  cannot be edited under a row that still describes the old one.

  Each folder also gained a CONTACT-SHEET.jpg: every image in it, numbered, as
  thumbnails. 856 KB for the whole library against 400 MB of originals. Each
  sheet carries a digest of what it was built from in its own JPEG comment, so
  C22 can tell a stale sheet from a current one without a second file to keep.

  Four folders that had no apparatus file got one: 02_printed-sheets,
  03_nameplates, 04_plant-room and 08_video. Every numbered folder now explains
  itself, and C24 keeps it that way: a folder of evidence with no note beside it
  is a folder only its author can use, and only for as long as they remember.

  Writing that up produced the twenty-fourth mistake immediately. The README was
  given the sentence "every numbered folder carries its own READ-ME.txt", which
  is false in four of them: 05_balance-room has an ANNOTATION-KEY.txt,
  10_manuals a SOURCES.txt, 11_products a CATALOGUE.txt, and 01_survey a
  DERIVED.txt. The sentence now describes the rule as it actually is, and C24
  checks the rule rather than the sentence.

  THREE THINGS THE AUDIT TURNED UP.

  02_printed-sheets/05 came off the phone tagged "Rotate 270 CW". The tag is
  correct and the pixels are the original bytes, so it renders upright anywhere
  EXIF is read and sideways anywhere it is not. It has NOT been re-encoded to
  straighten it: that would trade real provenance for the convenience of tools
  that are the ones in the wrong. Everything here reads the tag instead.

  07_drawings/01 carries the token t1946 while its capture time is 19:41. Both
  are real: the frame was shot at 19:41 and drawn on until 19:46, and the
  drawing is what the file is. Rather than rename a file three documents cite,
  the RULE was made exact, and MEDIA.tsv now carries both times so the token
  can be verified. C23 checks every token against the timestamps its file
  actually contains, which is what stops a plausible number being typed in
  during a rename.

  The two nameplates in 03 invite a wrong answer, and 03_nameplates/READ-ME.txt
  now says so out loud. The filter is rated 22 m3/h and the pump's curve never
  drops below 26, which looks like proof the bed is over-run. It is not: a pump
  plate says what the pump can do against a head, and what actually flows is
  set by the narrowest part of the circuit. The survey's equipment section had
  already worked that through and asks for one measurement, the suction pipe's
  outside diameter, which settles it. The READ-ME was written the wrong way
  round first and was corrected against the survey before either was committed.


  A NOTE ON WHERE THIS LANDED IN THE HISTORY.
  None of the above has a commit of its own. Two unrelated pieces of work were in
  the tree at the same time and two commits swept up both:

    ab96726  "Record that the overflow channel runs on two walls, not the
             perimeter" also carries the whole media library: MEDIA.tsv,
             tools/media.py, this file, the four folder READ-MEs, the seven
             contact sheets, the NAMING.txt rewrite, .gitattributes, and C21
             to C23.
    015c0cd  "Record the pool's plan shape, from a sketch, as topology only"
             also carries the survey's entire interface: the toolbar, the
             search, the key popovers and the photograph index. The survey HTML
             goes from 3,043,277 to 4,112,977 bytes in that one commit, and the
             message does not mention it.

  Both are pushed, so both stay: rewriting published history to tidy a message
  would be a worse trade than a message that under-describes its commit. This
  paragraph is the correction, which is the same way every other mistake in this
  repository is handled. An earlier version of it named only ab96726, which was
  itself the same error one level down.

  The lesson, and it is the same one this archive keeps learning: commit the
  thing you are describing, not everything the tree happens to be holding.

2026-09-08  DELETIONS
===============================================================================
  09_panoramas 01 to 03 were stitched from multiple hand-held frames and were
  wrong: one of them drew six drops on a wall that has four, and it did not
  look wrong. They were kept for a while as a record of how the survey was
  built, and that was the wrong call. A file that cannot be counted from,
  sitting in a folder of files that can, is a trap for whoever opens it next.
  The lesson was written into that folder's READ-ME and the images went.

  09_panoramas/04 was a genuine single frame and perfectly usable. It went
  because it was byte-for-byte identical to
  04_plant-room/20_electrical-panel-full_t1707.jpg, which is the original.

  04_plant-room/28 was a second frame of the pump strainer lid, shot in the
  same second as 27 and differing only by camera shake. They were not
  byte-identical, so the exact-hash audit that found the panorama duplicate
  walked straight past them. C19 now compares what pictures look like rather
  than what bytes they contain.

2026-09-08  THE MANUALS
===============================================================================
  10_manuals gained the four Maytronics documents for the robot cleaner, 06 to
  09. Note for anyone checking them: the X70's manual is badged "CLASSIC 8+ /
  TOP 8" and never names the X70. It is the right document; Maytronics badge
  the same chassis differently by market. SOURCES.txt records that, and the
  full part number the maker's portal needs, which is 99991034-MXI and not the
  short 99991034 that returns nothing.

  The robot's manual is also what narrowed the salt window. The printed sheets
  allow 4 to 9 g/L; the robot's hard ceiling is 5.0. The usable range for
  running both machines is 4.0 to 5.0, and that is now what the survey says.

2026-09-07  FOUR PASSES IN ONE DAY
===============================================================================
  FOURTH PASS, LATE
  09_panoramas gained four rectilinear reprojections of the 39 MP photosphere,
  05 to 08, and a READ-ME splitting the folder in two: the older stitches must
  not be counted or measured from, the reprojections may be. 12_scan took the
  next free number for a 3D scan of the room. The reading order therefore runs
  11_products then 12_scan, which is append order rather than thematic order.
  Folder numbers may be reordered when it is worth it; these two are not.

  THIRD PASS, EVENING
  The chemical stock arrived as 17 photographs and became 11_products, taking
  the next free folder number exactly as the padding was meant to allow. Filed
  by function, pH then chlorine then algae then clarity then booster then
  winter then salt, rather than in capture order, because that is how you reach
  for them.

  One source filename misled: "correttore ph+ & instructions" is not the back
  of the first pH+ tub, it is a second and different pH+ product, dosed at
  roughly four times the rate. FILENAMES ARE A CLAIM; THE LABEL IS THE
  EVIDENCE. That mistake is why 11_products photographs every product with its
  label rather than only with its front.

  SECOND PASS, EVENING
  Folder numbers were single digits while file indices were two. That breaks
  the moment there are more than ten folders, because "10_" sorts before "9_".
  Every folder was padded to two digits, so Products could arrive as
  11_products with nothing to renumber.

  A 07_drawings folder was added between the two rooms and the media that
  follows, and the plant room's dimensioned sketch moved into it: it had been
  filed as 04_plant-room/35, which made a folder of photographs contain
  something that is not one.

  FIRST PASS, AND WHY
  Six different naming grammars were in use across seven folders. All 56 files
  were renamed to one pattern, contents verified byte for byte unchanged. Two
  names were also corrected on the substance:

    04_plant-room/10  was "corner-probe-tube". It is the level column, not a pH
                      or ACL probe. The probes are on the POD at floor level,
                      seen in 04_plant-room/29.
    04_plant-room/34  dropped "by-owner" to match the survey, which no longer
                      attributes anything to a person.

  LATER THE SAME DAY
  Six photographs of the balance tank and its access arrived. A second room
  made "4_room" ambiguous, so it became 4_plant-room and the balance room took
  the next slot. Three folders shifted up by one to keep the two rooms adjacent:

    4_room      -> 4_plant-room
    5_video     -> 6_video
    6_panoramas -> 7_panoramas
    7_manuals   -> 8_manuals
    new folder   = 5_balance-room

  The restructuring was done once, before the products folder was filed, rather
  than twice. Those three then moved again in the SECOND PASS below, when
  07_drawings was inserted and everything after it was pushed up, which is how
  they reach today's 08_video, 09_panoramas and 10_manuals. The table is kept
  because an old citation is only decodable if the intermediate numbering is
  written down somewhere; condensing it to "three folders shifted up by one",
  as an earlier version of this file did, loses exactly the part you would need.
