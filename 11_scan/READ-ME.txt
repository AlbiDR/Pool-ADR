WHAT IT CONFIRMS

  It is to scale in metres, and it corroborates the tape independently:

      along the long axis    scan 2.52 m    tape 2.55 m
      along the short axis   scan 2.00 m    tape 1.96 m
      entrance notch depth   scan 0.40 m    tape 0.30 m

  Two independent methods agreeing to within a few centimetres is the useful
  result here. The notch also reads as a genuine absence of floor area rather
  than a recess in a wall, which is how the survey draws it.

  CEILING HEIGHT, PARTLY. The floor is a hard plane: one 2 cm slice holds
  59,000 vertices. Above it density tapers away and stops at 2.33 m, and the
  top 25 cm of the scan holds only 1.6 percent of all vertices. There is no
  ceiling plane, because the phone was never pointed up. So this does not
  close the measurement, but it does move it from unknown to AT LEAST 2.33 m,
  which puts the room's air volume at 10.5 m3 or more.

WHAT IT IS NOT GOOD FOR

  Counting or identifying anything. Thin objects do not survive a scan at this
  density: the manifold appears as a row of blobs, not as four countable
  drops. For that, use 08_panoramas/07, which is a reprojection of the
  photosphere and has been counted, or the photographs themselves.

  Positions. The scan's coordinate frame is arbitrary and its handedness is
  not established, so the plan above can be mirrored without contradicting
  anything in the file. The door position in the survey comes from the owner's
  direct testimony and was corrected twice; it has NOT been re-derived from
  this scan and should not be. To orient the scan properly one unambiguous
  anchor is needed, for example which corner the filter stands in relative to
  the door.

HOW THE FIGURES ABOVE WERE OBTAINED
  The GLB was parsed directly: JSON chunk for structure, BIN chunk for the
  POSITION accessors. Wall planes were found by histogramming vertex
  coordinates in 4 cm slices along each axis and taking the dense slices,
  a large flat surface being the only thing that puts tens of thousands of
  vertices in one slice. Nothing was estimated by eye.
