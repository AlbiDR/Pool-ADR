MANUFACTURER DOCUMENTATION
Downloaded 2026-09-07. These are the makers' own documents, not household
notes. Where they disagree with the printed sheets from the room, the survey
now says so explicitly and says which one it is following.

01_kripsol-sand-filter_manual_es-en.pdf     20 pp, Spanish + English
  Kripsol sand filter range, Q400 to Q900. Covers the vessel, the six-way
  selector valve, sand loading, backwash, rinse, initial start-up, wintering,
  and a fault table for both the equipment and the water.
  Source: https://cdn.hayward.fr/production/media/5/0/9/5/5e66b7.pdf
  (Hayward's CDN. Hayward owns Kripsol.)
  Key figures found:
    - Gauge reads 0.5 to 0.7 during normal filtration. NOTE: this is the
      filter in isolation. Measured on this installation 2026-09-09, clean
      sand freshly backwashed, the reading is 1.18 bar. The pump, the
      pipework, the cell and the throttled A all add head. Do not apply
      Kripsol's figure here.
    - When it passes 1.0, stop the motor and backwash. This is a trigger,
      not a target. The printed sheet in the room says "about 1 bar",
      which is the backwash point rather than the normal reading.
    - Backwash until the sight glass runs clear, two to three minutes.
    - Rinse is very brief. It recompacts the bed loosened by the backwash.
    - Always change valve position with the motor stopped. Press the handle
      down firmly first, it acts as a clutch, then move it.
    - Vessel rated for 1.6 kg/cm2 maximum working pressure.
    - Water fault table gives pH 7.2 as the target to adjust to.

02_zodiac-tri-chlorinator_manual_en.pdf     18 pp, English
  The TRi salt chlorinator itself: the controller head, the cell, salt levels,
  and the base error set (No Flow, Check Salt, Output Fault, Cleaning).
  Source: https://www.jacksonsleisure.com/content/pdf/salt_chlorinator/ZodiacTRi%20manual.pdf
  Key figures found:
    - Maintain salt above 4000 ppm, which is 4 g/L. The printed sheet in the
      room says minimum 4 g/L. They agree exactly.
    - Check Salt warns between 3000 and 4000 ppm depending on water temperature.
    - No Flow shuts production down automatically.
    - Cleaning means the cell is reversing polarity. Wait about five minutes.

03_zodiac-tri-pro-module_manual_es.pdf      20 pp, Spanish
  The TRi PRO module: pH and ACL (ORP) regulation, the peristaltic acid pump,
  the POD, probe calibration, and the pH/ACL error set. Manufacturer's own copy.
  Source: https://www.zodiac-poolcare.es/medias/product/Manual%20Modulo%20Tri%20Pro%20%20en%20castellano.pdf
  English edition, same document, readable online:
  https://www.manualslib.com/manual/2126989/Zodiac-Tri-Pro.html
  Key figures found:
    - The POD is one body carrying FOUR things: the flow detector that stops
      the cell without flow, the pH probe, the ACL probe, and the pH minus
      injection. Confirmed against photographs 04 and 29, which show exactly
      those four ports on its lid.
    - Placement rules, section 3.3, all of which this installation meets:
        horizontal pipe, so the probes hang vertical
        FIRST element after the pool filter
        before any heater or exchanger, so it reads unheated water
        at least 30 cm of straight pipe, and more than 20 cm from an elbow
        probe cables away from high-voltage supply cables
      The consequence worth writing down: the acid injects BEFORE the cell,
      and that is the designed arrangement rather than a fault.
    - The flow detector's arrow must be parallel to the pipe. It is.
    - "Las valvulas del by-pass de la celula TRi deben estar siempre abiertas":
      the cell's bypass valves must always be open.
    - Calibrate the probes at least once every two months during the season.
    - pH set point default 7.2. ACL set point default 1, of 6.
    - pH regulation is off by default and enables itself about 8 hours after
      power-on. A display reading "pH ---" means it has not enabled yet.
    - pH ERROR fires either when the pump has dosed a full set of cycles
      without reaching set point, OR when it has not run for over 72 hours.
    - ACL ERROR fires after 30 cumulative hours of production without
      reaching set point, or 30 hours without operating.

  Maytronics Dolphin Maximus X70 robot cleaner. Part number 8180035 Rev. 01.
  Source: the maker's own manual portal, indexed by the FULL part number:
  https://manuals.maytronics.com/intro/?pn=99991034-MXI
  (The short form 99991034 does not resolve. That is what defeated an earlier
  attempt to find this.)

  READ THIS BEFORE YOU DECIDE IT IS THE WRONG MANUAL. The document is badged
  "CLASSIC 8+ / TOP 8" and never says Maximus X70 anywhere in it. It is the
  right manual: Maytronics badge the same chassis differently by market, and
  the portal serves this file for part number 99991034-MXI.

  OPERATING LIMITS, from section 3. The robot may be used in these water
  conditions ONLY:
      Chlorine     maximum 4 ppm
      pH           7.0 to 7.8
      Temperature  6 to 35 C, and below 15 C climbing performance suffers
      NaCl         MAXIMUM 5,000 ppm, that is 5.0 g/L

  THE SALT LIMIT CONFLICTS WITH THE PRINTED SHEETS. They give a salt range of
  4 to 9 g/L and Zodiac asks for above 4 g/L. The robot tolerates at most 5.0.
  The usable window for running both is therefore 4.0 to 5.0 g/L, not 4 to 9.
  See the water section of the survey.

  THE CHLORINE LIMIT CONFLICTS WITH SHOCK DOSING. A commissioning shock at the
  granular chlorine's own rate of 10 to 15 g/m3 gives roughly 8 mg/L, double
  the robot's limit. The robot must be out of the pool for any shock and must
  stay out until free chlorine falls below 4.

  POWER SUPPLY. Digital switch-mode, IP54, input 100-250 VAC 50-60 Hz 180 W,
  output under 30 VDC, Bluetooth. Site it at least 3.5 m from the pool edge
  and at least 11 cm above the ground, roughly at the centre of the long side.
  No extension cords.

  MAINTENANCE that is easy to get wrong: do NOT let the cartridge filters dry
  out before cleaning them, so rinse them straight after the cycle rather than
  later. Do not force the filters back into place. The troubleshooting table
  attributes poor coverage to a badly sited power supply, too little cable let
  out, or cable kinks, before anything mechanical.

  Storage: upright on the caddy, out of sun, rain and frost, 5 to 45 C.

10_dab-eurocover-pump_manual_en.pdf         2 pp, 14 languages
  DAB EuroCover, the pump that sits on top of the winter pool cover and
  removes rainwater so its weight cannot break the cover. New to this
  archive 2026-09-24; not previously catalogued anywhere in this survey.
  Source: https://www.dabpumps.com/en_en/products/catalog/eurocover/eurocover
  Manual: https://www.dabpumps.com/content/dam/dabpumps-dam/products/dab/eng_master/swimming_pool_pumps/eurocover/documentation/r-d-documentation/instruction-manual/eurocover_dab_instruction_manual_printed_version_60121606.pdf

  WHAT IT IS, from the product page. Fully automatic submersible pump,
  built-in float switch, large support base for stability on an uneven
  surface. Thermoplastic body, stainless steel motor, shaft, bolts and
  screws. Threefold seal with an oil pre-chamber. IP68.

  FIGURES: flow rate 0.5 to 6 m3/h, head 6.5 m, liquid temperature 0 to
  35 C.

  NOT RATED FOR SALT WATER, in the manual's own words: "may not be used
  to pump salt water, slurry, flammable, corrosive or explosive liquids,
  grease, oils or food products." WORTH HOLDING ONTO ON THIS INSTALLATION
  SPECIFICALLY, since this is a salt pool: the pump sits on the cover and
  handles rainwater collecting on top of it, not pool water, so this does
  not apply in normal use. It would matter if the cover ever tore or
  overflowed enough that pool water reached the top surface.

  REMOVE BEFORE A FREEZE: "any ice could cause damage to the pump or
  float." Not automatic, a seasonal task to remember.

  MAINTENANCE: check it periodically while it is out, more often in
  rainy periods, and clear the suction screen with a water jet if leaves
  or debris block it.

  The downloaded document is a 2-page multi-language safety and quick-use
  sheet (IT, FR, GB, DE, NL, ES, SE, PL, SK, TR, RU, RO, GR), not a full
  technical manual; the figures above come from the product page itself,
  cited separately.
